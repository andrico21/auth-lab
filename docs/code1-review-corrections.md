## Two additional negative presets

`lab-kc-spnego-missing-acceptor-mic` and `lab-kc-spnego-invalid-acceptor-mic` make the F01 stop condition inspectable. They use an explicitly synthetic **standards negative SPNEGO profile**: Kerberos is offered/selected, the stated acceptor preference would favor another mechanism if present, and the first response declares `request-mic`.
