1.3.3 FAST
Feedback
Summarize this article for me
In this article
Flexible Authentication Secure
Tunneling (FAST) provides a protected channel between the client and
the Key
Distribution Center (KDC). FAST is only available for Authentication
Service (AS) and ticket-granting service (TGS)
exchanges.
FAST armor uses a ticket-granting
ticket (TGT) for the computer to protect Authentication Service (AS)
exchanges with the KDC, so the computer’s AS exchange is not
armored. The user’s TGT is used to protect its TGS exchanges with the KDC.
