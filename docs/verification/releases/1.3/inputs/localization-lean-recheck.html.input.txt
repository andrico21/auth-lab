<h2 id="performance-context">Performance context</h2>
<p>The worker’s current host measurements include Arabic UAE standalone
startup at <strong>6.61 s</strong> and first language switch at
<strong>5.35 s</strong> median. The previous candidate’s separately
collected medians were 18.66 s and 13.13 s, respectively. Those
earlier/current observations came from different collection epochs; they
suggest improvement but are not a controlled paired comparison between
candidates. The current measurements also include a Hebrew Compact
startup median of <strong>10.14 s</strong>, with high variability.</p>
<p>The 59 current flags and 866 prior flags remain separate, open and
unwaived. Qualified human language review, G01, external
browser/device/AT, Windows/DC, PAM and prior performance conditions
retain their previously disclosed status. No approval or waiver is
implied.</p>
